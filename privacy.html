Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information UGC Toolkit (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects when you visit our website and use the free UGC rate calculator, and how that information is used and shared. It is intentionally short, because we collect very little.

1. Information We Collect

  • Calculator inputs stay on your device. Everything you select or adjust in the calculator (platform, content type, experience, volume, follower count) is processed entirely in your browser. It is never sent to a server, and we never see or store it.
  • No accounts, no forms. We do not run user accounts and do not ask for your name or email address unless you choose to contact us.
  • Standard technical logs. Our hosting/CDN provider automatically records basic request data (such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps) for security and operations purposes.

2. Analytics and Advertising Cookies (Google Analytics 4)

  • We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (for example, page views and visit duration). GA4 may set cookies (such as _ga) to distinguish anonymous visitors.
  • We plan to display ads through Google AdSense in the future. When enabled, Google may set advertising cookies, including for personalized ads. For visitors from the European Economic Area, advertising cookies will only be set after you give consent through our consent banner (Consent Mode v2).
  • You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time, and manage personalized-ads preferences in Google Ads Settings. Blocking analytics or advertising cookies does not affect the calculator.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that operate this site:

  • Google (Google Analytics 4, and Google AdSense when enabled). Google’s use of data is governed by its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare (website hosting and CDN, processing request-level technical data).

We do not share your information with any other third parties, except when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of this site.

4. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability (GDPR, for visitors in the EU/EEA);
  • know, delete, and correct personal information, and opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of it (CCPA, for California residents — see the section below).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email in Section 8. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

  • We do not sell your personal information as that term is defined by the CCPA/CPRA.
  • For CCPA purposes, when personalized advertising through Google’s cross-context advertising (AdSense) is enabled, that constitutes a “share” of certain information (such as cookie identifiers) for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • California residents have the right to direct us not to sell or share their personal information (“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information”). You can opt out at any time via the footer link on this site, by blocking cookies in your browser, or through Google Ads Settings.
  • We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

6. Affiliate Disclosure

This site may contain affiliate links to third-party products and services. If you make a purchase through such a link, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Disclosures follow FTC guidance (16 CFR Part 255) and are also placed next to the relevant links.

7. Children’s Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
