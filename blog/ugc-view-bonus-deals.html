UGC View Bonus Deals Explained: $100 at 100K Views and Other Standard Structures

Last updated: 2026-11

You quoted a base rate. The brand countered with something extra: "We'll pay you a bonus if the video hits 100K views." That clause has a market rate — and most creators accept whatever number the brand offers, because they've never seen the ladder. This guide shows the standard view bonus structure, where these deals show up, the math on three worked examples, and the contract terms that decide whether the bonus ever gets paid — based on aggregated public benchmarks.

What Is a UGC View Bonus?

A view bonus is a performance bonus paid on top of your base rate when a video you delivered hits an agreed view milestone — usually after the brand puts paid spend behind it. The standard structure is a ladder: $100 at 100K views, $250 at 250K, and $500 at 500K.

The key words are "on top." Your base rate pays for production — concepting, filming, editing, delivery. The view bonus pays for outcome: the brand shares a slice of the upside when its paid campaign works. It is not a substitute for your base rate, and it is not a revenue share that replaces your fee when views don't arrive. A deal that pays "only if it performs" is not a view bonus deal — it's a deal with no guarantee, and you should price it as one.

One relative worth naming: influencer posting. That's when you publish to your own audience and charge a distribution fee for the reach you already own. A view bonus is different — the video usually runs from the brand's ad account, as a Spark Ad on TikTok or a partnership ad on Instagram, so the milestone measures the campaign's performance, not your follower count. A creator with 2,000 followers can earn the same view bonus as one with 200,000 — the brand's spend drives the views, not your reach.

What should you ask for at each milestone? The 2026 benchmark ladder pays $100 at 100,000 views, $250 at 250,000, and $500 at 500,000 — figures based on aggregated public benchmarks.

Read the ladder as three separate trigger points, not a cumulative schedule. Most deals pay the highest milestone the video reaches, not the sum of every milestone passed — and that interpretation is exactly what belongs in your contract, covered below. At the top of the ladder, $500 for a half-million-view ad creative is performance money the brand pays only after the ad has already earned it back.

The ladder is a reference structure, not a ceiling. Videos that run for months with heavy spend can pass 500K views easily; when a brand forecasts seven-figure reach, quote the ladder as a floor and negotiate the upper milestones before signing. After launch, you have no leverage to add them.

Notice also where the ladder starts: 100K. There is no standard bonus tier below that line. If the brand's planned spend makes 100K look unlikely, don't force a ladder onto the deal — price the certainty instead, with a higher base rate or a longer usage license.

Where View Bonus Deals Show Up

Which deals carry a view bonus? Based on aggregated public benchmarks, three situations show up most:

  1. Paid ad deals. Brands buying UGC to run as Spark Ads or partnership ads attach view bonuses most often, because paid spend makes the milestones both reachable and measurable.
  2. Performance-brand campaigns. E-commerce brands that judge creative by return on ad spend prefer outcome-linked pricing — the bonus aligns your incentive with theirs.
  3. Multi-video batch deals. When a brand orders three to five videos for testing, per-video milestones let it reward whichever creative wins, without re-negotiating the brief.

What you won't see: view bonuses on organic-only deals, where the brand posts to its own feed with no spend behind the video. Without paid distribution, hitting 100K views is a lottery ticket, not a pricing term — quote those deals on base rate and usage rights instead.

Brands propose the clause more often than creators do, but there is no rule that says you wait to be asked. Attaching the standard ladder to your next paid-ads quote is a low-cost move: it signals you know how performance pricing works, and it puts your milestones on the table before the brand's own version arrives at half the rate.

Negotiation Notes: Bonus on Top, Milestones in Writing

Two rules protect the money.

The bonus is an add-on, not a replacement. If a brand offers "$300 total, but $100 of it is a view bonus," that is a $200 guaranteed deal wearing a $300 costume. Your base rate is the floor; the ladder is upside. When a brand wants to shift risk onto you by swapping base pay for performance pay, the price of that risk belongs in the quote.

The measurement terms go in the contract. A milestone that isn't defined is a bonus that doesn't get paid. Four terms, written down:

  • Which dashboard counts. The brand's ad manager, the platform's public counter, or an analytics export — they disagree, sometimes by a lot.
  • The window. Milestones measured over 30 days pay differently than milestones measured over the life of the ad.
  • Stacking. Highest milestone reached, or the sum of every milestone passed? Both exist in the market; only one is in your contract.
  • Per video or per campaign. In a batch deal, bonuses attach to each video's own performance — not the campaign average.

Send the number with those terms attached, and the bonus stops being a promise. It becomes a line item the brand's ops team can pay without a meeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a UGC view bonus?

A performance bonus paid on top of your base rate when a video you delivered hits an agreed view milestone, usually after the brand puts paid spend behind it. The standard ladder pays $100 at 100K views, $250 at 250K, and $500 at 500K.

How much is a view bonus at 100K views?

$100, based on aggregated public benchmarks. At 250K views the standard bonus is $250, and at 500K views it's $500.

Is a view bonus paid instead of my base rate?

No. The bonus is an add-on. Your base rate pays for production and is guaranteed; the view bonus is upside that triggers if the video performs. Never swap base pay for performance pay at the same total.

How are views counted for a view bonus?

However your contract says. Define which dashboard counts, over what window, whether milestones stack, and whether bonuses are per video or per campaign — the brand's ad manager and the platform's public counter often disagree.

Do I need followers to earn a view bonus?

No. The video usually runs from the brand's ad account as a Spark Ad or partnership ad, so the milestone measures the campaign's performance, not your reach.
